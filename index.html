About

More than
spreadsheets.

Nash & Co is an accounting and finance practice based in Isleworth, London. We are led by Nisha Hansewraj — an accountant with a first-class degree in Accounting and Finance from London South Bank University and part-qualified ACCA status.

We work with individuals, freelancers, and small to medium-sized businesses, providing hands-on support with bookkeeping, tax, VAT, and financial reporting. Every client's situation is different, and we tailor our approach to fit yours.

Our team is fluent in Sage, QuickBooks, Xero, and Excel, and we are happy to work with whatever system you already use. Files can be shared easily via email or Dropbox — no complicated setup required.
