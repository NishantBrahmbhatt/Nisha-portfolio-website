About

More than
spreadsheets.

Hi, I'm Nisha. I'm an accounting and finance professional based in Brentford, London, with a BA Hons in Accounting and Finance from London South Bank University — where I was awarded Top Student for three consecutive years.

I work with individuals, freelancers, and small to medium-sized businesses, offering hands-on support with bookkeeping, tax, VAT, and financial reporting. Every client's situation is different, and I tailor my approach to fit yours.

I'm fluent in Sage, QuickBooks, Xero, and Excel, and I'm happy to work with whatever system you already use. Files can be shared easily via email or Dropbox — no complicated setup required.
