About

More than
spreadsheets.

Hi, I'm Nisha. I've spent the better part of a decade helping people make sense of their finances — from sole traders filing their first self-assessment to small business owners planning for growth.

I believe good accounting is about more than compliance. It's about giving you a clear picture of where you stand, and the confidence to make better decisions from there.

Based in London, I work with clients across the UK — remotely or in person, whatever works for you.
